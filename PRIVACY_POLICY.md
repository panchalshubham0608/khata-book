# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how the Khata Book app uses Google Drive and Google Sheets when you choose to back up a report.

## Information collected
When you use the backup feature, the app requests access to your Google account to create or update files in Google Drive and Google Sheets on your behalf. The app may access:
- the report title and report data you are backing up
- the list of categories attached to each expense
- the Google Drive folder and spreadsheet files created for backup purposes

## How the data is used
The data is used only to:
- create a folder named "KhataBook" in your Google Drive if it does not already exist
- create a spreadsheet for the report and add a new backup sheet for each backup action
- store the report content in that spreadsheet for your personal use

We do not use your backup data for advertising, resale, or unrelated analytics purposes.

## Storage and sharing
- Backup data is stored in your Google Drive / Google Sheets account.
- The app does not upload your report data to any third-party server other than Google APIs required for the backup feature.
- The app does not share your report content with other users unless you explicitly choose to do so through the app's existing report-sharing features.

## Your choices
You can revoke the app's Google Drive and Google Sheets permissions at any time from your Google account settings. If you revoke access, future backup actions will stop working until permission is granted again.

## Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the project maintainer through the repository issues page.
